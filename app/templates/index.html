The game of HIPE was described by Peter Winkler in his excellent collection of mathematical puzzles.

The rules are straightforward: you are given a string of letters, and your goal is to find an English word that contains those letters as a substring. So if the string were "HIPE", the answer might be "arcHIPElago".
